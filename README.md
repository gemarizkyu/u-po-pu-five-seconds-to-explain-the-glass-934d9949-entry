## What this is

An entry for the make.fun bounty **"Five Seconds to Explain the Glass"**: one sentence from a house cat, defending itself five seconds after sweeping a glass off the table. No apology, no confession — the cat treats the whole thing as an eviction, not an accident:

> In my defence, that glass was squatting in my sunbeam like it paid rent, so I evicted it — the floor is its new address now.

The sentence is 124 characters, inside the bounty gate of 1–200 characters. The angle is deliberately fresh: instead of the usual gravity, slippery edge, or "it was already falling" excuses, the cat claims the glass was trespassing in its sunbeam — territorial, unrepentant, and spoken in five seconds flat.
